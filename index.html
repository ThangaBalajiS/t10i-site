Who am I?

I'm a builder at heart and a full-stack engineer by trade. I've spent the last 8+ years shipping production software on the MERN stack, most recently as a lead engineer taking features from idea to production at scale.

These days a lot of that work involves AI — orchestrating LLMs into real products, building MCP servers, and wiring up agentic workflows. But my favorite kind of engineering is the kind nobody assigns me: the browser extension I built because my own tabs were a mess, the invoicing tool I made because I got tired of ugly PDFs.

When I'm not building, I'm usually with my son Paari, or chasing one of my many Side Quests (scroll down — they get weird).

Infinite Tabs Manager

A privacy-first browser extension that turns tab chaos into calm.

Save, group, and restore your tabs from one minimal dashboard — and cut browser memory usage by up to 96%. Smart grouping bundles tabs by site (or dumps them all in a click), and audible tabs are automatically skipped so your music never stops. Built for Chrome and Firefox, with zero data collection.

1,000+ weekly active users up to 96% less memory
